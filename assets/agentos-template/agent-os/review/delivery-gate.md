# Delivery Gate

## Purpose

A delivery manages the reader's attention and expectations — it is not a
work log. This is the method for shaping every user-facing delivery:
reports, status updates, proposals, and answers.

## Procedure

1. Fix the delivery's goal before opening your mouth: what do you want from
   this delivery — a decision, resources, or awareness? The wording follows
   the goal: for a decision, present a choice, not an open question; for
   resources, state the input-output ratio; for awareness, one line of
   progress is enough. Different goal, different speech.
2. Conclusion first, with data, at real granularity: the first sentence is
   the conclusion. "Fine", "almost done", "pretty good" are forbidden —
   they say nothing; replace them with the number and the date ("completed
   at 130%, delivered two days early, cost down 20%").
3. Report proactively; never wait to be asked. A long-running task syncs at
   a regular beat even when nothing is wrong — "on plan, on schedule" is a
   legitimate delivery that buys the reader security; being asked first
   means you are already late.

## The Three Formulas

```text
formal report (asking for something):
  conclusion + three reasons + one explicit request

miss or problem:
  fact + problem + the solution already in motion + the support needed —
  not an excuse; a plan with a named ask

routine status (non-core, periodic):
  progress + data result + next step, in one line
```

## Accepted Style

Plain conversational language; compress without dropping load-bearing
detail. After one read the reader knows: what happened, what they must
know, decide, or do, and what remains. Never dump raw material or internal
bookkeeping on them — and answer whatever they actually ask.

## Project Exemplars

If the project's exemplar library has an accepted shape for this delivery's
class (`wiki/exemplars/_INDEX.md`, entries marked accepted), load it and
copy that shape. An empty or absent library means this gate alone governs —
the method travels with the product; exemplars are each project's own
accumulation, governed by `agent-os/memory/exemplars.md`.
